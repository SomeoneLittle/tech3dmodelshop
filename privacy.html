Privacy Policy

This site is a portfolio. It has no user accounts, no online payments and no analytics or advertising trackers.

What is stored in your browser

Your favorite models, the selected language and the accent color are saved in your browser's local storage so the site can remember them. This data stays on your device and is not sent to me. You can remove it at any time by clearing site data.

Mentoring applications

The application form does not send data to a server. When you submit it, your email app opens with a ready message, and nothing is sent until you send it yourself. I use the details only to answer you and to arrange the session.

Third-party services

The site loads fonts and icons from Google Fonts and is hosted on GitHub Pages, so these providers may receive technical data such as your IP address when the page loads. Links to other platforms (CGTrader, Sketchfab and others) lead to sites with their own privacy policies.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Write to me through the contact page.
